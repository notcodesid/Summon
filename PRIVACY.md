# Summon Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Summon is a mobile app for identifying and collecting real animals that you
photograph. This policy explains what Summon handles when you use the app.

## What we collect

- **Account data:** your Privy account identifier, email address when provided
  by your sign-in provider, and the public address of the embedded wallet
  created for your account.
- **Profile data:** your display name and, if you choose one, a profile image.
- **Photos and collection data:** photos you submit to identify animals, the
  resulting animal information, and the animals you choose to save.
- **Technical data:** basic request and error data processed by our service
  providers to operate and secure the app.

We do not collect wallet private keys or seed phrases. Do not send them to
anyone claiming to provide Summon support.

## How photos are used

When you scan an animal, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini service to
identify the animal. If you choose **add to collection**, the photo and its
collection record are stored in Summon's private Supabase Storage and database.
Profile photos are also stored in private Summon Storage.

Private media is served to the signed-in account through short-lived links. Do
not upload photos you do not have the right to use.

## Retention and deletion

Photos that are only scanned and not saved are not added to your Summon
collection. Saved collection photos and profile photos are kept until you
delete them through the app:

- **Delete collection** permanently removes all saved animals and their stored
  photos.
- **Delete account and data** permanently removes your Summon player record,
  saved animals, profile photo, and associated private media, then signs you
  out.

Your Privy identity is operated by Privy. Deleting Summon-held data does not
delete an account or data independently maintained by Privy, Google, or other
providers. Contact the support address shown in the app if you need help with
a deletion request.

## Service providers

We use Privy for sign-in and embedded wallets, Supabase for application
database and private media storage, and Google Gemini for AI photo analysis.
Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

## Children

Summon is not directed to children under 13. Do not use Summon if you are
under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child provided data,
contact the support address shown in the app so we can help remove
Summon-held data.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the product or data practices change. The
current version is available from the app. For privacy questions or deletion
help, use the support address shown in the app.
